Every Session Has Purpose.

A neuro technical training environment that builds the complete modern player. Here is what happens in a 60 minute session.

OmniCage · ROXPro Lights
The Method

Three phases. One hour. Zero wasted reps.

Most training stops at technique. The lab is built so that technique gets tested under the same pressure your player faces on Saturday, and then measured.

01

High frequency stations

Passing, first touch, dribbling, finishing and agility in rotating stations. 700 to 1,000 quality touches per player. Both feet, every session.

02

Cognitive load under pressure

Inside the OmniCage and on ROXPro reaction mats, light signals force players to scan before the ball arrives and decide in a split second. This is where speed of play is built.

03

Measure, benchmark, re test

Speed, reaction time, scanning and accuracy are tracked live. Coaches correct on the spot. Every 8 sessions we re test, so parents and players see the progress in numbers.

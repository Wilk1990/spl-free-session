Questions parents ask

Is the session really free?

Yes. The regular price of a session is $75. New players enter the code FREESESSION at checkout and pay $0. One free session per player.

Who can join the free session?

Boys and girls born 2008 to 2017, ages 8 to 18, who have never trained with Soccer Performance Lab. Every level is welcome, from first club season to academy.

My child has little soccer experience. Is that a problem?

No. Groups are built by age and level and the coach adjusts each station. Some experience helps players get the most out of the session, but beginners train here every week.

Can I stay and watch?

Yes. Parents are welcome for the whole session, and after the session the coach will talk you through what they saw.

What should my child bring?

Indoor soccer shoes or turf cleats, a water bottle and athletic clothing. Shin guards are optional. Please arrive 5 minutes early so the group can start on time.

What happens after the free session?

Your coach recommends the training option that fits your player's age, level and goals. Regular groups run Monday to Friday, sorted by birth year. You decide if and when you want to continue.

What does training cost afterwards?

A drop in session is $75. Monthly passes bring the price per session down. Your coach walks you through the options after the trial, and there is no obligation to continue.

My child is younger than 8. Can they come?

The free trial session covers ages 8 to 18. For younger players we run Little Ballers on Saturday mornings. Email info@soccerperformancelabinc.com and we will find the right spot.

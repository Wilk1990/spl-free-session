This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through the Site, our lead forms and call-booking calendar, and our related communications and business operations. It does not apply to information you provide directly to an unaffiliated third-party website or service under that party’s own privacy policy; however, this Privacy Policy describes our use of personal information we disclose to service providers that process it on our behalf and to third parties that may process it for their own purposes. Those parties maintain their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

By using the Site, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy; where applicable law requires consent for a particular practice, we will obtain that consent separately. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Site.

3. Cookies and Similar Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. We and our service providers use cookies, web beacons, pixels, tags, local storage, server-side conversion tools, and similar technologies for: (a) operating and securing the Site and lead forms; (b) remembering preferences and consent choices; (c) analyzing Site traffic and visitor interactions; and (d) measuring, attributing, and optimizing advertising campaigns, including determining which advertisement, campaign, keyword, or referring source generated a visit or lead.

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings, and you can generally set your browser to notify you when a cookie is placed. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Site from working. Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies and will honor your consent choices.

5. Marketing Communications and Call Booking

If you submit a lead form, book a call, or otherwise request information, we may store your contact details, submission or appointment details, source and date of the inquiry, communications, and marketing preferences in GoHighLevel or a similar customer relationship management, email, and scheduling platform. Our emails may contain tracking pixels and tagged links that record whether a message was delivered, opened, or clicked, and the general device and location from which it was opened. We use this information to manage and follow up with leads, administer appointments, measure engagement, and improve our marketing and services.

You may opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in the message or by contacting us under Section 20, and you may opt out of marketing text messages, if any, by replying STOP. After you unsubscribe we may retain a record of your email address on a suppression list so that we do not email you again.

Text Message (SMS) Program Terms and Compliance

If you check the SMS consent box on one of our forms, you agree to receive text messages from City of Angels Advertising LLC at the phone number you provided. We offer two separate consents, and you can give either, both, or neither:

  • Non-marketing messages about your Ad Strategy Session: appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling updates, and customer support communications.
  • Marketing messages about special offers, discounts, and service updates.

Opt-in and consent: you will only receive messages if you have explicitly opted in through a dedicated checkbox on our web forms. We maintain timestamped records of all opt-in actions and comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), CTIA guidelines, and all applicable laws. You must be 18 years or older to participate in our SMS program.

Opt-out: you can cancel SMS messages at any time by replying "STOP" to the number that sent you the message. You will receive a final confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again. All opt-out requests are processed immediately.

Message frequency and content: message frequency varies based on your interactions with our business. Non-marketing messages relate directly to the services you have requested. We do not send promotional content without your specific consent.

Help and support: reply "HELP" for assistance or contact us at info@cityofangelsadvertising.com. Customer support is available during regular business hours.

Carrier information: standard message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers include AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and most regional U.S. carriers.

SMS data protection: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share your phone number with SMS aggregators and providers solely for the purpose of delivering messages you have consented to receive. We implement strict data protection measures to safeguard your SMS opt-in information and consent records. Our full SMS terms are also available in our Terms of Service.

Questions we hear a lot

We already have an agency for Google. Is this different?

Yes. Google catches people who already search for a dentist. Meta reaches the people who need an implant and haven't started looking yet. Video is how they get to know you before they call.

Do you need our doctor on camera?

It works best when the doctor is in it, even for 20 minutes. We do the rest with your team and your rooms. We direct everything, nobody needs to be a natural.

Are before and after photos allowed?

Meta limits them, so we don't build ads around them. We tell the story with the doctor and the patient instead. That also keeps your ads from getting rejected.

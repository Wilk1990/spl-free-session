Why most agencies can't fill a calendar (and why they don't tell you)

Reason 1

They never shoot anything.

Stock footage and templates. People scroll past what they've seen a hundred times. Nobody books a $3,000 service from a stock photo of a smiling model.

Reason 2

They test one idea a month.

Meta rewards volume. One creative a month is not a test, it's a guess with your money on it. Winners come from 15 to 20 variations, not one.

Reason 3

They report clicks, not bookings.

"Leads" is a vanity number. The only numbers that matter are what a booked appointment costs you and how many of them showed up.

Reason 4

You met the founder. You got the intern.

The person who sold you is not the person running your account. By month two, your business is a ticket in somebody's queue.

An ad agency that doesn't shoot, doesn't test and doesn't track is a design studio with a Meta login.

We do the opposite of all four. Not because we're smarter, but because we built our own business this way first, with our own money on the line. That changes how you work.
